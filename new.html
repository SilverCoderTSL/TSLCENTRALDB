THE GOLDEN RULES

01 / KEEP IT SEPARATE

Your MAP and STATS dispatches are separate. Don't combine them.

02 / USE THE GENERATOR

Let the tools produce the code for you. Manual editing is unnecessary unless you know exactly what you're changing.

03 / BALANCE MATTERS

The numbers are broad RP indicators, not a perfect simulation of every factory, tank and accountant in your nation.

04 / HAVE FUN

TSL is a place to build interesting nations, not a competition to produce the world's most aggressively boring spreadsheet.

01

🗺️ STRATEGIC MAP & BORDERS

Define where your nation exists. Draw your territory, add disconnected islands or territories, and plant your capital where your government insists it belongs.

LAUNCH MAP TOOL >
// PROCEDURE
  1. Click DRAW BORDER MODE in the top bar.
  2. Click SET CAPITAL and place your capital city on the map.
  3. Click points across the grid to trace your land boundaries.
  4. Use FINISH ISLAND when tracing extra disconnected territories.
  5. Click COPY JSON to grab your encoded map snippet.
  6. Paste the snippet into a NationStates Factbook dispatch titled strictly MAP.
FIELD NOTE // Don't worry about making the map look like a perfect GIS survey. The goal is a clear, usable representation of your nation's territory.
02

📑 NATION STATS GENERATOR

Turn your idea of a country into a consistent set of RP statistics. You have room to make a small peaceful republic, an industrial powerhouse, a technological monster—or something considerably more questionable.

LAUNCH STATS GENERATOR >
// PROCEDURE
  1. Complete the 4-part wizard: Identity, Economy, Tech, RP.
  2. Keep your settings within the system limits: Max 100M Population and Max 7 Power Points.
  3. Review your choices before compiling. A powerful nation should have a reason for being powerful.
  4. Click COMPILE BBCODE when you're satisfied.
  5. Click COPY TO CLIPBOARD.
  6. Paste the output into a separate NationStates Factbook dispatch titled strictly STATS.
POWER POINTS // Think of the 7 points as broad indicators of national capability, not literal units of military power or percentages of GDP. Spend them deliberately—being amazing at everything is usually less interesting than having strengths, weaknesses and a reason for both.
// POWER_POINTS

SEVEN POINTS. USE THEM WISELY.

The current system gives nations a maximum of 7 Power Points. That's enough to create meaningful specialization without turning every country into an unstoppable superstate with infinite money, infinite technology and somehow still a functioning public transport system.

●●●● ●●● MAXIMUM: 07

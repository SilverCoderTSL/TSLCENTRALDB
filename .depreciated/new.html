⚠️ DISPATCH TITLES: USE "MAP" FOR BORDER CODE & "STATS" FOR STAT CODE ⚠️

🗺️ 1. STRATEGIC MAP & BORDERS

LAUNCH MAP TOOL >
  1. Click DRAW BORDER MODE in the top bar.
  2. Click SET CAPITAL and place your capital city on the map.
  3. Click points across the grid to trace your land boundaries.
  4. Use FINISH ISLAND if tracing extra disconnected territories.
  5. Click COPY JSON to grab your encoded snippet.
  6. Paste the snippet into a NationStates Factbook dispatch titled strictly MAP.

📑 2. NATION STATS GENERATOR

LAUNCH STATS GENERATOR >
  1. Complete the 4-part wizard (Identity, Economy, Tech, RP).
  2. Keep your settings within limits: Max 100M Pop & Max 7 Power Points.
  3. Click COMPILE BBCODE at the end.
  4. Click COPY TO CLIPBOARD.
  5. Paste the output into a separate NationStates Factbook dispatch titled strictly STATS.

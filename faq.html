SYSTEM OPERATIONS & PROTOCOLS FAQ

Welcome to the Tactical Mapping and Regional Intelligence documentation hub. Below are standard operating procedures for regional registrations, border dispatch codes, nation sheet submissions, and automated map updates.

⚠️ MANDATORY DISPATCH TITLES: USE EXACTLY "MAP" FOR BORDER Snippets & EXACTLY "STATS" FOR STAT QUESTIONNAIRES ⚠️

To register your nation on the global strategic vector map, follow these steps[cite: 10]:

  1. Open the STRATEGIC MAP tool[cite: 10].
  2. Click ✏️ DRAW BORDER MODE at the top menu[cite: 10].
  3. Click 📍 SET CAPITAL to mark your capital city location[cite: 10].
  4. Click consecutive points around your border on the map grid to trace your land boundaries[cite: 10].
  5. If your nation has islands, click ➕ FINISH ISLAND and trace additional landmasses[cite: 10].
  6. Click COPY JSON to encode your coordinate payload into your clipboard[cite: 10].
  7. Post the generated snippet inside your Nation Factbook thread titled MAP[cite: 10]!

To register your nation's political, economic, and strategic statistics in the regional database:

  1. Navigate to the Nation Character Sheet Generator tool.
  2. Complete the Power Budget Questionnaire (Max 7 points allocated across Economy, Tech, Resources, Infrastructure, and Defense).
  3. Click GENERATE BB CODE and copy the resulting BBCode block.
  4. Dispatch a separate Factbook thread titled STATS on NationStates.
  5. The automated sync script runs weekly to parse, audit, and display your nation's profile on the Analysis & Roster page.

Copy the code generated by either system and paste it directly into a new dispatch post on NationStates[cite: 10].

CRITICAL REMINDER: Your post title must be set strictly to MAP for border vectors[cite: 10] or STATS for statistics. Any other title will be ignored by the automated parser.

The code output contains an anti-tamper payload generated via TSL_Integrity[cite: 10]. This ensures that border vectors and spatial coordinates cannot be manually forged, modified, or altered outside of the mapping tool interface[cite: 10].

Updates are completely automated via GitHub Actions! The workflow automatically executes every week (Mondays at 03:00 UTC) to scan the region's dispatches, rebuild region-map-data.json and region-stats-data.json, and update the live site.

Simply re-run either tool, generate a fresh payload snippet, and edit your existing MAP or NATION SHEET dispatch post with the updated data payload[cite: 10]. The weekly automation pipeline will pick up your changes during the next run.
